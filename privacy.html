Privacy notice

Revoke access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future API access, not retained copies. The owner can remove credentials and delete local history, files and memories through private controls or server administration. Provider-held copies follow provider deletion and retention rules.

This site contains no tracking scripts or Google account content. Its host may process ordinary request logs.
